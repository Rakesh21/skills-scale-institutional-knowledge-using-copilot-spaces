# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A central index for the OctoAcme project management process documents.

## Project management processes

OctoAcme follows an iterative project lifecycle. Initiation validates the business need, goals, success measures, stakeholders, risks, and resource needs before a decision to proceed. Once approved, planning turns the initiative into prioritized, estimable backlog items with acceptance criteria, owners, dependencies, milestones, a Definition of Done, and an initial QA approach.

During execution, teams deliver small increments, track work on a project board, and coordinate through standups, planning, reviews, and demos. The Project Manager (PM) coordinates schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications; the Product Manager (PdM) defines outcomes and prioritizes work; developers build and test; QA validates acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Clear ownership and collaboration support progress from backlog through review, QA, and completion.

Communication is maintained throughout the lifecycle: the PM and PdM align weekly, delivery teams use standups and regular delivery syncs, and stakeholders receive periodic or milestone-based updates. Teams keep project status and risks visible, review the risk register regularly, and escalate blockers from team-level triage through the PM and Product Lead to the sponsor when needed. Security incidents follow the security incident runbook and notify Security on-call.

Quality is built into delivery through unit and applicable integration tests, CI checks for tests and linting, security scanning, code review, and manual QA when needed. Before release, teams confirm acceptance criteria, passing CI and security scans, release notes, smoke tests, and a rollback or mitigation plan; they then verify the deployment and communicate the release. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents capture lessons and track a small number of improvement actions with owners and due dates.
