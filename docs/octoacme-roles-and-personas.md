# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Test Lead

### Role Summary
QA or Test Leads define how quality is validated and ensure releases meet the agreed acceptance criteria and user expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and release readiness criteria
- Coordinate automated and manual test planning across project milestones
- Verify acceptance criteria with Product Managers and stakeholders
- Identify quality risks, defects, and operational impacts early
- Partner with Developers to improve testability, automation coverage, and bug triage

### Goals
- Reduce the risk of defects reaching production
- Improve confidence in release quality and regression prevention
- Ensure quality is a shared responsibility across the delivery team

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and defect triage updates
- Quality status in sprint reviews and release readiness checkpoints
- Partnering with Product Managers on acceptance validation and with Developers on fixes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to define test cases and ensure the product is built to be testable.
- Collaborates with Product Managers to confirm that user-facing outcomes and acceptance criteria are being validated.
- Reports risk and release quality status to the Project Manager so dependencies and escalations are visible.

---

## UX / Product Designer or Researcher

### Role Summary
UX Designers or Researchers help translate customer problems and user needs into clear experiences, workflows, and product decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research, interviews, and usability feedback collection
- Define interaction flows, UX requirements, and design decisions
- Work with Product Managers to validate problem framing and opportunity areas
- Partner with Developers and QA to make sure designs are feasible, testable, and consistent with requirements
- Help identify trade-offs that affect usability, accessibility, and adoption

### Goals
- Improve user experience and product usability
- Align product decisions with real customer needs and evidence
- Reduce rework caused by late design feedback or unclear requirements

### Typical Communication
- Discovery and user research summaries
- Design review sessions and workflow feedback loops
- Collaboration with Product Managers and Developers throughout delivery

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Product Managers by clarifying customer problems, success metrics, and user journeys.
- Provides design direction and trade-off guidance to Developers while helping QA validate usability and acceptance criteria.
- Keeps Project Managers informed of design dependencies, review timing, and any risks that may affect milestones.

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide the technical approach, system design, and delivery standards that keep the project maintainable and aligned to business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define architecture, technical standards, and integration patterns
- Review design choices for scalability, maintainability, and security
- Help estimate complexity and identify technical dependencies early
- Guide technical trade-offs and design decisions with Product and Project leads
- Support Developers in technical problem solving and design review

### Goals
- Deliver sustainable technical solutions that support long-term product value
- Reduce avoidable technical debt and architecture drift
- Improve decision quality across cross-functional teams

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Dependency and risk discussions with Project Managers and stakeholders
- Guidance during planning, implementation, and releases

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to translate business goals into technical constraints and trade-offs.
- Works with Developers on implementation quality, review standards, and technical risk mitigation.
- Advises Project Managers on dependencies, estimate confidence, and technical blockers affecting schedule.

---

## DevOps / SRE / Operations Lead

### Role Summary
DevOps, SRE, or Operations Leads ensure that software can be reliably delivered, observed, and supported in production environments.

### Responsibilities
- Define deployment pipelines, environment standards, and release practices
- Support monitoring, alerting, incident response, and operational readiness
- Help teams build for scalability, reliability, and observability
- Partner with Developers and QA to validate production-readiness requirements
- Participate in rollback, contingency, and incident coordination planning

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risks and downtime
- Improve operational visibility and response time
- Ensure the service is production-ready and maintainable after launch

### Typical Communication
- Deployment readiness reviews
- Production incident coordination and postmortems
- Operational dashboards, alerts, and release checklists

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and QA to ensure features are deployable, observable, and testable in staging and production-like environments.
- Supports Project Managers by identifying deployment dependencies, rollback considerations, and operational risks.
- Aligns with Product Managers on release timing, customer impact, and readiness to support new functionality.

---

## Business Sponsor / Stakeholder Representative

### Role Summary
Business Sponsors or Stakeholder Representatives provide strategic direction, funding support, and executive alignment for the initiative.

### Responsibilities
- Define business priorities, sponsorship commitments, and decision authority
- Support issue prioritization, cross-team alignment, and resource planning
- Review progress against strategic outcomes and business value
- Help resolve escalated conflicts, funding questions, or organizational dependencies
- Champion stakeholder communication and alignment across the project lifecycle

### Goals
- Ensure the project remains aligned to business value and executive priorities
- Enable timely decisions that keep momentum and reduce delays
- Support sustainable outcomes that extend beyond delivery of a single feature

### Typical Communication
- Executive briefings and milestone reviews
- Sponsorship updates and decision points
- Escalation channels when risk, scope, or priority decisions require leadership input

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to confirm business outcomes and success metrics and to resolve prioritization trade-offs.
- Receives project status and risk summaries from Project Managers so decisions are based on the latest information.
- Supports teams by providing strategic backing and faster decisions when issues cross organizational boundaries.

---

## Role Flexibility for Smaller Teams

On smaller projects, one person may cover multiple roles. For example, a Developer may also serve as Technical Lead, or a Product Manager may also handle UX research and stakeholder communication. The goal is not to assign every persona as a separate full-time role, but to make sure key responsibilities are clearly owned even when responsibilities are combined.

This flexibility helps teams stay accountable without creating unnecessary overhead while preserving clarity on ownership, decision-making, and communication.
